---
title: "Gingkos"
date: Fall 2025

They planted hope too shallow,
roots gasping beneath packed soil.
No one knew yet
how water listens to depth.

The gingkos bent like students
waiting for instruction,
their gold unpromised,
their silence misunderstood.

One by one they vanished—
emptied squares where learning once stood.
But the earth remembered.

Now every autumn,
the gingkos glow like forgiveness,
their leaves falling all at once,
a quiet applause
for what we finally learned
to grow.
