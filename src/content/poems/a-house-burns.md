A house burns
without asking who lived there.

Down the road,
a car rests on its spine,
wheels turning
out of habit.

I keep moving
staircases that
rise
only
to
stop.

A door waits,
sealed,

Above me,
the sun forgets its name.
The moon stays.

Time folds.
Nothing opens.
This is what remains.
