---
title: "6:10 — wake you, gentle."
date: Fall 2025
order: 6
page: ../../assets/poems/six-ten.png
---
6:10 — wake you, gentle.
6:25 — pack rice box - one egg, soy sauce drizzle.
6:35 — wake you again. still sleeping.
your hair sticks to your cheek like seaweed.
6:50 — bus late again, sky still gray.

7:15 — drop you off, you wave but don't look back.
8:00 — clock in. smell of acetone sharp in the morning.
8:30 — soak stranger's feet, thick skin peeling soft as fruit.
smile anyway.
10:30 — hold a stranger's hand longer than my own child's.
11:00 — small talk, smile through headache.
11:15 — clip nails, cracked yellow moons. hold breath.

12:30 — eat in back room, rice cold, scroll through photos.
1:00 — bleach burns through glove.
3:15 — customer snap fingers — you missed a spot.
4:45 — numb fingers, wrists ache, back burns.
6:10 — last one wants coffin tips, long, sharp.
7:00 — count cash, tip box light again.

7:10 — bus back, dark already.
8:00 — home. dinner quiet.
no talking. only the sound of metal spoons.
8:30 — fold clothes, wash hands still smelling of lemon.
8:45 — text you: "study hard, ok?"
8:46 — delete the message.
you already know.

9:00 — set alarm.
sleep.
repeat.
