---
title: "Golden Glow"
date: Fall 2025
order: 1
page: ../../assets/poems/golden-glow.png
---
it starts
with sirens—
always does.

red streaks
from the Arch,
smoke rises
knife sharp
into
the humid dark.

alley shadows
grate,
laughter
dissolves,
midnight
melts.

I look
for Jack.
ache
like
cheddar.

kitchen corner—
plastic moonlight.
a wedge in my hand,
golden
glowing
almost
warm.

the city hums,

Unbothered.
