2:00:00

I flip pen, click cap
doodle shapes
white spaces.

I stare ceiling—
the ceiling stares back,
grid blank squares

1:58:59

And writing
while same equations,
while page sweats hand,
while clock continues march,
while pen borrowed
someone smarter,

while flip formula sheets,
while professor looms
shadow behind me,

while pulse races
faster minute
while brilliance
a story
I tell myself,

while I wait
ceiling
answer my call.

"Pencils down!"

0:00:00
