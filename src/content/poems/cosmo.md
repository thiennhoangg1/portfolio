---
title: "Cosmo"
date: Fall 2025
order: 5
page: ../../assets/poems/cosmo.png
---
I cook and clean as though you were my wife.
I keep the lights low,
waiting
for your return.
the night expands—
its planets leaning in,
as if to watch
the orbit
of your grace.
You sleep all day,
only awake
when I lie down.
At night
you climb my chest
and
claim my breath.
The lamp goes dark
yet still
I feel your stare,
a steady watch.
I call your name—
you barely turn your head.
no thanks,
no word
of what I've done.
And yet,
I love
your silence.

love,
me
