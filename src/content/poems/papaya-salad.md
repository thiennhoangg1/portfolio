---
title: "Papaya Salad"
date: Fall 2025
order: 2
page: ../../assets/poems/papaya-salad.png
---
The pitter-patter
of drops splatters as we
walk through the night
market - our last dance under
twilight's wane,
out of step with the rain.

The torrent acidic, almost bitter,
hisses on concrete.
Sapphire light fractures.
June bugs scatter.
Cold finds skin.
Humidity hums, then pauses.

The deluge has the courage to say
what our eyes don't. We feign
ignorance, splashing in puddles to
remind us of forgiving times.

A flash of greased sprites spark,
splitting the sunken sky. When
lightning strikes, that's who
decides.

A tinge in your eye I could not
control. Damp and sullen, we tread
until water reaches our knees. A swell of
evaporation follows lingering smells
of lemon basil, matchsticks of sweet,
salty fish sauce and sour tang.

Once sultry, now still, under
the weeping sky. The words
grow thin.

The monsoon in
May is unforgiving,
unlike green papaya salad
in the rain.
