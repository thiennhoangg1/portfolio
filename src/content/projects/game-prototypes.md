Weekly builds from a video game programming course, each one focused on a single piece of how games work.

- **Movement:** character control and feel.
- **Physics maze:** rigidbodies, colliders, and maze obstacles.
- **Platformer (parts 1 and 2):** jumping, level design, and hazards.
- **Space shooter:** ships, projectiles, and waves of enemies.
- **Audio, pausing, and menus:** music, sound effects, a pause screen, and menus.
- **Cameras:** following, framing, and transitions.
- **Adventure (parts 1 and 2):** a top-down, Zelda-style world built from tilemaps.
