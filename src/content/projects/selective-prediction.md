Graduate research with Prof. Athena Tabakhi on selective prediction and abstention: mechanisms that let a model say "I'm not sure" instead of guessing.

I implement and evaluate deep learning classifiers on the EuroSAT remote-sensing dataset, then analyze their confidence, calibration, and abstention behavior to ask when deferring is the ethical choice.
