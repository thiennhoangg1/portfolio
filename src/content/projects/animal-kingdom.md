Animals drop into a pit and roll, stack, and bump into each other. When two of the same animal touch, they merge into the next, bigger animal. Let the pile cross the line and it's game over.

You can play it in the Arcade in drive mode (press D, then find the neon sign).

## What I built

- **Merging:** the animal sprites and the logic that combines two matching animals into the next level up.
- **Game over and win screens:** the end-of-game flow and its UI.
- **Underwater world:** a second world with its own water physics, plus the UI for switching between land and sea.

My teammates added weight differences between animals, sound effects and music, bombs, and the instructions screen.
