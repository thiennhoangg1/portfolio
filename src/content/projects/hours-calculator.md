A local web app and command-line tool that totals regular hours, overtime, and pay for any period.

- Tracks wages and raises per employee; hours over 40 a week are paid at 1.5×.
- Enter shifts one by one or as monthly totals.
- **Scan a photo** of a paper time card and the app reads the punches for you to review before saving. It works from a phone on the same Wi-Fi.
